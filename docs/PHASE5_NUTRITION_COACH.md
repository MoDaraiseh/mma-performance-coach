## Architecture and data flow

- `ingredients.py` and `app/data/ingredients.json`: food values and source metadata. `tools/import_usda.py` reproduces the subset from a downloaded official archive without network access.
- `dietary.py`: explicit exclusions and common allergy aliases; no LLM interpretation of restrictions.
- `nutrition_calculations.py`: documented resting equation, heuristic activity/goal adjustments and macro guidance.
- `meal_composition.py`: bounded portion solver, source/macro energy arithmetic, groceries and validation.
- `nutrition.py`: seven-day composition and validated substitutions.
- `nutrition_integrity.py`: source snapshots, effective calculation mass, freshness/review status and proposal bindings.
- `nutrition_models.py`: strict nutrition settings, changes and coaching intent contracts.
- `coach_actions.py`: reduced context, offline interpretation and supported edits with availability/equipment/logged-session checks.
- `mma_agent.py`: explanation/intent provider transport, deadlines, retries, output checks, fallback and usage metadata.
- `main.py`: authenticated orchestration and serialized approvals using the existing versioned aggregate repository.
- `NutritionPage.tsx`, `NutritionWorkbench.tsx`, `Coach.tsx`, `Progress.tsx`: integrated dashboard, feedback/catalog, full candidate review, approval and reassessment status.

No migration beyond **0004** is required; new aggregates use the existing versioned record schema. Existing profiles and plans are retained. Old illustrative nutrition snapshots are labeled stale and withheld until a reviewed current-input version is generated. Startup does not silently replace personal nutrition data. Existing training and fighter workflows are preserved.

## Calculation and source boundaries

The [methodology](METHODOLOGY.md#nutrition-phase-5) gives equations, thresholds and limits. Default is maintenance. Optional goal adjustments require an explicit nutrition setting and are limited to the smaller of 5% or 150 kcal; reductions have additional recovery/BMI/competition/hunger exclusions. No rate of loss, rapid cut or target competition weight is promised. Minors and specified clinical needs receive general guidance without numerical plans. These bounds are app policies, not medical clearance or a validated weight-management protocol.

Protein guidance and lower-volume carbohydrate/fat context are linked to primary scientific sources in the [source register](SOURCES.md#phase-5-nutrition-and-coaching-sources-reviewed-2026-10-31). Exact targets, activity factors, uncertainty band and portion choices are transparent product heuristics. The Mifflin estimate is not a measured basal/resting metabolic rate; the activity product is not measured TDEE.

The catalog was extracted from the [official USDA archive](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/fdc-datasets/FoodData_Central_sr_legacy_food_csv_2018-04.zip), whose [data terms](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-guide/) state public-domain/CC0 use. It contains rice, lentils, chickpeas, potato, broccoli, banana, olive oil, chicken breast, firm tofu, oats, quinoa, black beans, Greek yogurt, egg, carrot, tomato, apple, orange, bulgur, couscous, spinach and salmon. Per-record FDC IDs and original preparation descriptions are retained. The archive is historical; observation in 2026 does not make the original composition values newly measured.

Source kcal are retained independently from the app's macro-derived `4P + 4C + 9F` display energy. Fiber and food-specific energy factors explain why they need not match. Source numbers are not altered to conceal that difference. The solver uses protein/carbohydrate ingredients plus fixed produce, then balances measured oil. Portion limits and daily macro/energy tolerances are checked. Grocery quantities sum named raw/dry/cooked edible states; package counts and cooking yields are not inferred.

Allergy metadata and cost tiers are editorial, not USDA product certifications. Halal poultry requires appropriate sourcing/certification; no generic record certifies a shopping item. Dietary additions never relax profile restrictions. Unrecognized allergies or infeasible safe portions withhold plans. Vegan/vegetarian, dairy-free and gluten-free screening includes conservative oat exclusion where applicable. Cultural settings adjust ingredient ordering and are explicitly “inspired” patterns. Legacy free-text preferences remain visible as a limitation; structured dislikes/unavailability provide reliable exclusions. Micronutrient adequacy, fiber optimization, food quality and GI tolerance remain professional-review needs.

## User workflows and API contracts

**Nutrition:** open Nutrition → optionally save explicit preferences/feedback → preview updated meals or a selected meal substitution → inspect all seven days, portions, macros and updated groceries → approve or reject. Feedback is user input, not approval of a generated plan. Rejection leaves the active record unchanged; if its dietary context is stale, it remains withheld. Approval atomically appends a new nutrition version and resolves the proposal. Histories remain read-only and are included in JSON account export.

**Coach:** ask a supported question/change → local rules or the optional provider interprets → backend checks current data → a persisted preview appears with before/after details → user approves/rejects → successful approval saves a new version. A chat reply does not itself modify a plan. A request for an absent weekday, logged/historical session, unavailable exercise, stale nutrition or unsafe context produces an explanation instead of a fabricated action. Ambiguous unnamed-exercise requests ask for clarification. Shortening changes only the specified future week/session, not all recurring weekdays; the preview identifies the week. Boxing requests can focus an existing solo block without adding load or claiming a coached class. Existing coached classes require coordination with the instructor.

| Endpoint | Contract |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /api/nutrition` | Current version; stale contexts expose guidance/status rather than old numerical targets/meals |
| `POST /api/nutrition` | Existing explicit authenticated manual generation/regeneration API retained for compatibility; app UI uses preview/approval for existing-plan edits; AI has no access to this as a write tool |
| `GET /api/nutrition/catalog` | Source-backed generic catalog and energy/metadata caveats |
| `GET/PUT /api/nutrition/preferences` | Versioned explicit feedback; validates exclusions; never edits athlete profile or applies a generated plan |
| `GET /api/nutrition/history` | Account-scoped immutable saved meal-plan versions |
| `GET/POST /api/nutrition/proposals` | Reviewed regeneration or meal-substitution previews |
| `POST /api/nutrition/proposals/{id}/{approve\|reject}` | Own pending proposal only; exact bindings and deterministic recalculation checked on approval |
| `POST /api/coach` | Persists labeled explanation and optional validated preview reference; no automatic plan application |
| `GET /api/coach/actions` | Account-scoped coaching previews and their current resolution status |
| `POST /api/coach/actions/{id}/{approve\|reject}` | Revalidates intent/candidate and source/log bindings; appends training/nutrition version only after approval |

Approvals bind profile, approved training, current nutrition, preference and check-in IDs; coaching actions also bind lineage workout IDs. A source change during the provider call prevents preview creation. Any later bound-context change prevents applying an old preview. The existing account write lock serializes version creation and decision checks; simultaneous nutrition approvals produce one saved version. Training continuations preserve previous weeks, cycle lineage and logged performance; profile inputs are never overwritten by the proposed duration or calculated nutrition mass.

## Reassessment

Nutrition review is triggered by relevant profile fields (including goals/availability), explicit restrictions/preferences and changed approved weekly training volume. A plan ID change with equal volume does not itself continuously change calories. Three recent measurements spanning at least 14 days, all at least 2% in the same direction, can support a review; isolated weight variation does not. After approval, the calculation may use the check-in median while retaining the original profile snapshot. The UI identifies that calculation mass. No logged workout automatically adds calories or replaces meals. The progress reassessment report links these review needs back to Nutrition for explicit preview and approval.

## AI reliability, privacy and cost controls

Official [OpenAI structured-output guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/structured-outputs?api-mode=responses) informs the Responses schema and refusal/incomplete handling. The model returns an explanation plus a nullable whitelisted intent; it never supplies trusted nutrient values, arbitrary program edits, tools, SQL or executable code.

Server-only key, fixed HTTPS endpoint, validated model name, `store: false`, maximum 16,000 serialized input characters and 1,100 output tokens. Reduced context includes a selected approved week and current nutrition summary, not entire plan histories or raw body measurements. Names, country and recovery free text are not automatically forwarded; user question/goal prose can contain personal information. The frontend's optional provider toggle explains the data sent.

Maximum two attempts: one retry for a connection failure or 429/503 with acceptable short retry delay (at most one second). Reads time out after 12 seconds, connect/pool after three, write after five, with a 20-second overall deadline. Invalid configuration/context, refusal, incomplete JSON, schema errors, unsupported claims, unknown numerical claims or provider failures use deterministic local fallback. Numbers in user prose are not treated as validated nutritional facts. These text checks are defense in depth, not proof that every generated explanation is semantically correct.

Sanitized usage records contain attempt count, elapsed time, bounded input/output token counts and outcome; logs exclude keys, questions, names, measurements and raw provider output. Conversation content is intentionally stored in the user's own account history and export. No live price/cost estimate is invented. There is no production-wide quota, persistent rate limiter or comprehensive medical-language classifier. Real provider latency, billing, account permissions and response quality remain unverified; all configured-provider QA uses synthetic transport responses.

## Remaining limits and next work

- This is a general educational macro/meal template, not a medically validated diet, complete micronutrient plan, GI-tolerance optimizer or competition camp service. Qualified sports dietitian/medical input is required for relevant clinical and competition-specific needs.
- Twenty-two historical generic ingredients and simple original bowl combinations; no comprehensive cuisine library, branded certification, local product availability, cooking-yield conversion or live price data. Exact meal selection remains bounded by catalog and portion feasibility.
- Optional energy adjustments and review thresholds are conservative product heuristics, not predicted results or a guarantee of sufficient energy availability.
- Offline language interpretation covers explicit supported requests; ambiguous context needs clarification. Unsupported recurring-calendar rewrites and unscheduled/coached-class changes are not silently improvised. No automatic chat-driven profile or restriction edits.
- Live paid AI, real account/billing limits, PostgreSQL, other browser engines, physical devices and manual screen-reader review remain unverified. Production identity, authorization, quotas, retention policy and provider-budget enforcement are future work.

Next priorities: independent sports-dietitian/MMA-coach review, a broader legally usable ingredient/recipe catalog with micronutrient and palatability checks, optional local price/product data, production provider quotas/observability, and broader device/assistive-technology validation. No arbitrary overall completion percentage is assigned.
