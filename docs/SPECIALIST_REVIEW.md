# Qualified sports and nutrition review checklist

Status: **not yet performed by qualified professionals**. Automated calculations, tests and browser checks cannot establish coaching efficacy, injury safety, dietary adequacy or medical suitability. Record reviewer name/qualification, date, fixture/profile, plan version, finding, severity, decision and follow-up for each item below. Do not place identifiable athlete medical data in a public issue.

## MMA coach

- [ ] Check beginner, experienced, returning and limited-access profiles against realistic discipline priorities; anthropometry must not imply fighting skill or determine aptitude.
- [ ] Review boxing, wrestling, BJJ, clinch and transition drills for supervision, progression, equipment, contact and sparring boundaries.
- [ ] Verify that solo substitutions do not imply permission for uncoached partner/contact training, and that coached-class edits refer back to the instructor.
- [ ] Review weekly technical load alongside actual club sessions, recovery and user enjoyment/access; verify missed sessions do not produce catch-up overload.
- [ ] Check that UFC comparisons explain limited physical resemblance, historical data, missing fields and differences without implying professional proficiency.

## Strength and conditioning professional

- [ ] Review exercise descriptions, modification notes, alternatives, RPE/rest guidance and available-equipment screening.
- [ ] Review relative strength/endurance/aerobic/explosive/mobility assessments; confirm recorded observations are distinct from estimates and population comparisons have appropriate evidence.
- [ ] Assess workload caps, session-duration estimates, 12-week phases, deloads and progression after success/failure/absence.
- [ ] Examine fatigue/sleep/soreness/readiness rules as conservative subjective coaching prompts, not diagnoses or validated recovery prediction.
- [ ] Review weeks 4/8/12 comparisons and whether proposed changes respect logged history and actual user constraints; approvals must remain explicit.
- [ ] Define referral pathways for pain, injury, unexplained fatigue, cardiovascular symptoms and return-to-sport needs. The catalog is not injury-specific programming.

## Sports dietitian / relevant medical professional

- [ ] Review Mifflin resting estimate, activity multipliers, maintenance band and selected metabolic input limitations; no estimate should be described as measured expenditure.
- [ ] Evaluate protein targets, carbohydrate availability under MMA load, dietary fat, recovery timing and risks of inadequate energy availability.
- [ ] Independently review the optional ±5%/150 kcal adjustments, exclusion conditions and sustained-weight review threshold. These are product heuristics, not validated clinical protocols.
- [ ] Assess seven-day portions for palatability, cultural appropriateness, GI tolerance, food safety, fiber and micronutrient adequacy. Current macro matching does not establish dietary completeness.
- [ ] Check cooked/raw/dry serving states, source versus 4/4/9 energy, groceries and feasible preparation/yields. No package shopping quantity is inferred.
- [ ] Verify vegan/vegetarian, gluten/dairy exclusions, common allergy handling and product/cross-contact checks. Generic USDA foods cannot certify halal or allergen safety.
- [ ] Review withholding/referral for minors, pregnancy/breastfeeding, eating-disorder concerns, clinical needs, low BMI/body-fat coverage and competition-specific changes.
- [ ] Define specialist-controlled handling for competition camps, weigh-ins, hydration and weight cuts. The application does not prescribe dehydration or rapid cuts.

## Shared review and sign-off

- [ ] Phase 10: review context provenance, missing/stale observations, discipline priorities, progression explanations and deterministic fighter/nutrition claims. Automated mock evaluations do not establish live coaching quality.
- [ ] Independently red-team untrusted prose, primary-request permission checks, ownership/version gates and premature-persistence claims. Review model/provider configuration, privacy/retention and actual costs before enabling live evaluations.

- [ ] Phase 9: review the 40-food snapshot, optional nutrient units, generic allergy screening versus product uncertainty, batch-preparation assumptions, increased variety and rebalanced food replacements. Verify realistic portions and dietary adequacy independently.
- [ ] Review training/recovery timing thresholds. Define future strength/endurance/day-specific targets only with adequate workload information; automatic calorie cycling is not implemented.

- [ ] Use realistic mixed fixtures: beginner with two short sessions, experienced athlete with club training, vegan/soy-free budget constraints, high hunger/poor recovery, missed sessions and changed restrictions.
- [ ] Inspect offline and provider-generated explanations against actual saved context; unsupported advice must be identifiable and correctable.
- [ ] Confirm that no chat statement is presented as a persisted change before approval succeeds; rejection and stale approvals retain history.
- [ ] Agree on escalation wording, supported population, monitoring and periodic source/catalog review.
- [ ] Publish a bounded approval decision with unresolved issues; do not substitute automated QA results for this sign-off.
